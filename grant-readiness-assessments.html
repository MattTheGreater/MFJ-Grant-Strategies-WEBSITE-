The problem

Most organizations lose before they apply.

Organizations tend to hire a grant writer after a loss and wonder what went wrong with the application. Usually the application was not the problem. The strategy was: the wrong funder, the wrong timing, or an organization not yet positioned to be competitive.

There is also a quieter risk. Some organizations win funding they are not yet ready to administer, and the award becomes a compliance liability. Readiness is about both ends of that: whether you can win, and whether you can manage what you win.

Our approach

An honest read, not a sales pitch.

  • Capacity. Staffing, systems, and the practical ability to carry a competitive application and then administer the award.
  • Financial health. The financial footing a funder looks for, and the controls a federal award will require.
  • Program outcomes. Whether your results tell a story a scoring panel will reward.
  • Documentation. The records and registrations that need to be in place before you can compete or draw down.
  • Fit and timing. Which programs you are genuinely positioned for now, and which should wait.

In practice

A typical engagement.

Example scenario

An organization is eager to pursue a large federal program it saw announced. The readiness assessment finds its program outcomes are strong but its financial controls and registrations are not yet where that award would require. We flag the gaps, identify two smaller programs it can win now, and lay out what to fix before the larger one, so it builds toward the big opportunity instead of losing on it. This is an illustrative example of the kind of work we do, not a specific client account.

FAQ

Common questions

What is a grant readiness assessment?+
A structured evaluation of whether your organization is prepared to pursue and manage funding, looking at organizational capacity, financial health, program outcomes, and documentation. The result is an honest picture of what you are ready to compete for and where the gaps are.
Why assess readiness before applying?+
Most organizations lose before they apply, not because of the application but because of strategy: wrong funder, wrong timing, or a position they were not yet ready to win from. A readiness assessment catches that before you spend time and money chasing a long shot.
What do I receive from the assessment?+
A readiness report with gaps identified honestly, priority programs you are positioned to pursue, and a documentation checklist so you know what to shore up first. It is a plan you can act on, whether or not you engage us further.
