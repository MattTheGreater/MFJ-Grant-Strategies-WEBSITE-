The problem

Research administration is a specialized load.

Federal research funding carries rules that most grant frameworks never touch: facilities and administrative cost rates, effort certification, cost sharing, program income, and sponsor-specific terms, all running across many simultaneous awards. A single misapplied indirect rate or a late effort certification can cascade into questioned costs.

Large institutions carry whole sponsored-programs offices to manage this. Smaller colleges and emerging research programs often do not, and they feel the strain the moment federal research dollars arrive.

Our approach

Treat the portfolio like a research office would.

  • Pre-award support. Budget development, cost-share planning, and compliance review before submission.
  • Award setup. Each award loaded with its correct indirect rate, terms, and reporting calendar.
  • Effort & cost share. Effort certification and cost-share tracking kept current and documented.
  • Subrecipient monitoring. Agreements and monitoring for the partners research awards so often involve.
  • Compliance & closeout. Uniform Guidance compliance, program-income handling, and clean closeout across the portfolio.

FAQ

Common questions

What is sponsored-programs administration?+
Sponsored-programs administration is how a university manages externally funded research and projects, from proposal and budget development through award setup, financial compliance, effort reporting, and closeout. It spans a pre-award office and a post-award office working together across many awards at once.
How is research grants management different from other federal grants?+
Research awards add layers such as facilities and administrative (indirect) cost rates, effort certification, cost sharing, program income, and research-specific terms, often across dozens or hundreds of simultaneous awards. The volume and the specialized rules are what make research administration its own discipline.
Can you support a small research office?+
Yes. Smaller institutions and emerging research programs often lack a full sponsored-programs office. We can serve as fractional research-administration capacity, or help a growing office build the systems it needs to scale.
