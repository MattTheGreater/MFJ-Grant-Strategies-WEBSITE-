Services

Federal grants management, compliance, and strategy.

MFJ Grant Strategies provides federal grants consulting across the full award lifecycle: deciding what to pursue, building competitive applications, administering awards under 2 CFR 200, and preparing for the audit. Engagements are billed as flat fees or a monthly retainer, never on contingency. How engagements work

Three ways to work with us.

Project

A defined deliverable at a flat fee: a readiness assessment, a compliance review, a policy set, a single application, a closeout.

Retainer

The fractional grants department. A monthly arrangement sized to your portfolio that covers strategy, proposals, administration, and compliance.

Advisory

Hourly or blocked time for an organization with its own grants staff that needs a second set of eyes, a specific question answered, or training.

No contingency fees. Prospecting, review, and proposal work are billed the same whether or not the award lands. Ongoing administration of an active portfolio is a separate engagement and, where appropriate, can be supported through an award's indirect-cost budget.
