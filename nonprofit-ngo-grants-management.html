The problem

One grant is impact. One source is a risk.

Many nonprofits chase grants one at a time, in isolation, and lean heavily on a single large funder. When that source shifts, the gap is immediate. Add the compliance load that federal and large private awards carry, including reporting, allowable costs, and subrecipient monitoring, and a small team can spend more time defending funding than delivering on it.

International NGOs face the same obligations, often across difficult operating environments and with funds passing to local partners. The administrative risk is real, and it is rarely where the mission-driven energy wants to go.

Our approach

Diversify the base, administer it cleanly.

  • A funding ecosystem. Federal, state, and foundation funding aligned into one strategy, so awards support one another rather than compete.
  • Clean administration. Reporting, allowable-cost tracking, and procurement handled to the standards each funder expects.
  • Subrecipient monitoring. Agreements and risk-based monitoring for funds passed to partners.
  • Compliance & audit readiness. Uniform Guidance compliance and the records trail a Single Audit will examine.
  • Capacity that lasts. Systems and training so the organization grows more self-reliant, not more dependent on a consultant.

FAQ

Common questions

Why do nonprofits need grants management beyond grant writing?+
Winning a grant creates obligations: reporting, allowable-cost tracking, procurement standards, subrecipient monitoring, and audit readiness. For a nonprofit, mishandling those can mean questioned costs or a lost funder relationship. Grants management is what protects the funding after it is won.
How can a nonprofit diversify its funding?+
By aligning federal, state, and private foundation funding into one coherent strategy so no single source can leave a gap when it shifts. A diversified base is more resilient than chasing one large grant at a time.
Do international NGOs face the same compliance rules?+
International NGOs that receive federal assistance carry award administration, subrecipient monitoring, and compliance obligations much like domestic recipients, sometimes with additional requirements. We help them manage those obligations across the award lifecycle.
